# GoldHour playbook — next 30 days

This is for you, not for the photographer. Do not put it in the product.

## This week (done in the app)

A photographer can run **one wedding**:

1. Save an enquiry (name, phone, date, venue, events, budget, source)
2. Quote → PDF + WhatsApp
3. Follow up in 2 days, then 5 if silent
4. Accept → collect advance → Book
5. Track Advance / Before wedding / Final delivery
6. Fill the 20 Dec timeline

## Week 2 — find 20, get 5

Do not say “please test my SaaS.”

> I'm building a simple studio desk specifically for wedding photographers. I'm looking for 5 photographers to use it on their real bookings for free and give me feedback. I don't need any payment or commitment.

Mix:

- 2 photographers (solo)
- 1 videographer
- 1 small studio
- 1 established wedding team

Where: Instagram (`Madurai wedding photographer`), Facebook groups, Google Maps, WhatsApp business groups, local exhibitions.

Send: https://kuttenajith.github.io/goldhour/

## Week 3 — watch, don't ask “do you like it?”

Sit with them (or a screen share). Say:

> You've received a new wedding enquiry. Show me how you normally handle it.

Then ask them to do the same job in GoldHour.

Write down every hop: WhatsApp → notes → calendar → Excel → PDF → UPI screenshot.

## Ten questions

1. How many wedding enquiries per month?
2. How many become bookings?
3. What do you use to track them today?
4. Where is client information stored?
5. How do you create quotations?
6. What part of managing a wedding is most annoying?
7. What do you repeatedly ask the client?
8. What do you forget most often?
9. What causes you to lose money or time?
10. If GoldHour disappeared tomorrow, what would you miss most?

## Week 4 — only the top 3 requests

Build nothing they did not touch.

Then ask: would you pay ₹500–₹1,000/month for this?

## Do not build yet

Google login, Razorpay, PostgreSQL, AWS, Docker, RBAC, a mobile app, AI, analytics dashboards, assistant logins.

Those wait until a studio says they have been using it for three weeks and want to continue.
